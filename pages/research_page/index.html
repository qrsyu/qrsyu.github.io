What is the nature of reality experience? A key question of my current research is how the brain generates visual experience from the inside, and what makes such experience feel "seen", or even real. Visual imagery, dreaming, and hallucinations are all examples of internally generated visual experience. They are vivid and perception-like but without external visual input. Besides, human spatiotemporal perception is another important aspect of reality experience. Our perception of space and time could be simulated during dreaming and distorted during hallucinations. Understanding the circuit mechanisms that allow the brain to generate such experience is essential for understanding the reality experience of the mind and its relation to consciousness. I use theoretical modelling (e.g., dynamical systems) with neural and behavioural data to approach this.
